# Product

## Product Purpose
Internal SaaS intelligence tool for DNS reconnaissance. Subdomain enumeration, CNAME resolution, and reverse DNS lookups against live infrastructure.

## Users
Security researchers, penetration testers, and DevOps engineers who run domain reconnaissance as part of their workflow. Technical users comfortable with CLI tools but wanting a faster visual interface.

## Register
product

## Brand & Tone
- Professional, utilitarian, dense
- Confidence without flash
- Tool-first: every pixel serves the task
- No marketing language in the product UI

## Anti-references
- Vercel dashboard (too much whitespace, too polished-consumer)
- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero sections
- Neon-on-black "hacker" aesthetic
- Corporate enterprise dashboards with excessive chrome

## Strategic Principles
- Information density over decoration
- Immediate utility on load
- Keyboard-first interactions
- Results are the hero, not the branding
